Grid & Grain · County Kits

One page each. Bring it to the meeting.

Checklists for a real decision: a hearing, a co-op meeting, a permit review.

Hold the draft ordinance, tariff, or agreement next to the kit and check off what it already covers. What’s left is what to ask for in writing. Each kit opens with its Field Guide module.

  1. 01 · Jan
    Know Your Utility

    A one-page fact sheet about your own co-op or utility: who owns it, board elections, rate classes, large-load rules, the connection line.

    Open now

    Large-load tariff checklist

    Six terms that keep a big new customer's costs off everyone else's bill.

    Open now

    Water disclosure checklist
