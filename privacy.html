Privacy

What we keep, and how to delete it

Short version: your email, your self-check scores, and the choices you make. We don’t sell or share any of it, and you can delete all of it yourself.

What we store

  • Your email address, to send your sign-in link and, if you ask for it, one note a month.
  • Your self-check scores: which module, the score, and when.
  • Your choices: that you confirmed you’re 13 or older, whether you want the monthly note, and, if you pick one, which group describes you.
  • Your certificate, if you earn one: the name, county, and state you type in. Anyone with its link can see those three things and the date.

We don’t ask for a password, phone number, address, or anything else. Reading the Field Guide never requires an account.

Who handles it

Accounts and scores are kept with Supabase. Sign-in links and the monthly note are sent through Resend. Both process data only to run this service for us. The site is hosted on Vercel.

What we never do

We don’t sell, rent, or share your information with data center developers, utilities, advertisers, or anyone else. Grid & Grain takes no money from developers or utilities.

Ages

Accounts are for people 13 and older. If you believe a younger child has an account, email us and we’ll delete it.

Deleting your data

Go to My account and choose Delete my account. Your account, every score, and your certificate are removed right away. You can also email ruralamerica56@gmail.com and we’ll do it for you. Every monthly note has a one-click unsubscribe.
