Grid & Grain · Straight Talk

What people say about data centers, graded.

Each claim in the speaker’s words, a verdict, the evidence, and what to ask for in writing.

We grade the claim, never the person. When the critics are right, we say so first. Every number names its source and date.

VERIFIED: a cited source directly supports it PLAUSIBLE: reasoned from evidence, not measured SPECULATIVE: an idea worth testing
  1. #01

    “Data centers are why my power bill went up.”

    Studies of 2015 to 2025 find small effects on home bills, sometimes lower prices. The risk is real where power is already tight.

    Overstated, with a real local riskVERIFIEDPLAUSIBLE for the future
  2. #02

    “A data center will drain our water.”

    A large data center can use millions of gallons a day; one closed-loop design plans for about what 65 homes use. Ask which kind before you argue.

    It depends on the coolingVERIFIED
  3. #03

    “It'll bring hundreds of jobs.”

    Construction jobs are many and temporary. Operating jobs are few, often fewer than 100, and specialists often come from outside the county.

    Wrong for permanent jobsVERIFIED
  4. #04

    “It will pay for our schools.”

    Data centers can carry a big share of a county's tax base. Tax abatements can give much of it away.

    Partly rightVERIFIED
  5. #05

    “They're 96 decibels, 24 hours a day.”

    Readings next to machines aren't levels at a home. But neighbors have sued over round-the-clock noise, and some cities now cap it at the property line.

    Overstated as a blanket claim; noise complaints are realVERIFIED
  6. #06

    “AI already uses most of America's electricity.”

    Data centers used 4.4% of U.S. electricity in 2023. But they cluster, so one utility's share can be far larger.

    Wrong nationally; local share is what mattersVERIFIED
  7. #07

    “There's nothing we can do; they'll build anyway.”

    States, co-ops, and counties are setting terms, and some are saying no. Zoning, tariffs, benefit agreements, and co-op elections are all levers.

    WrongVERIFIED
  8. #08

    “AI does nothing for rural people.”

    AI tools are already easing the load on doctors at some rural hospitals. But few rural hospitals use them yet.

    Wrong, but adoption is thinVERIFIED
  9. #09

    “Data center power use will double forever.”

    Efficiency once kept data center power use nearly flat while computing grew. AI changed that, and forecasts now span almost a factor of two.

    Plan in rangesVERIFIED
  10. #10

    “These deals are done in secret.”

    Local officials and residents have been asked to sign nondisclosure agreements, and secrecy is a leading driver of opposition.

    Often rightVERIFIED
  11. #11

    “Data centers can never cut back.”

    Some can, some of the time, and the contract has to say how much.

    OverstatedVERIFIED
  12. #12

    “My farm data is mine automatically.”

    Industry principles say farmers own their data, but principles aren't law. The agreement you sign decides who can collect, use, share, and keep it.

    It depends on what you signedVERIFIED
