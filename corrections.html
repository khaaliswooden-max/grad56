Grid & Grain · Corrections

What we got wrong, and when we fixed it.

We correct errors within 48 hours and list every one here, newest first.

Each entry shows what we said, what it says now, and the date we fixed it. Nothing is edited quietly, and no entry is ever removed from this page.

How corrections work

  1. 48 hours. Once we confirm an error, the page, post, or module is fixed within 48 hours.
  2. In the open. The fix is logged here with the original wording, so you can see exactly what changed.
  3. Sent to members. Each month’s note lists that month’s corrections.
  4. What counts. A wrong number, a wrong grade, a wrong source or date, or wording that misleads. Typos are fixed without an entry.

See something wrong? Email ruralamerica56@gmail.com with the page and the sentence. A person reads every one.

4 entries. Newest first.

  1. Fixed November 26, 2026

    Loudoun County’s data center tax share now comes from the county’s budget

    What we said
    VERIFIED In Loudoun County, Virginia, data centers generate almost 50% of property tax revenue, as reported in a trade-press review of the county’s proposed fiscal 2026 budget.
    What it says now
    VERIFIED The tax on data center computer equipment alone brought in $685 million in fiscal 2024, about 29% of the county’s local tax revenue. The county’s budget doesn’t separate out real estate tax on the buildings, so the full share is higher.
    PLAUSIBLE Counting the buildings too, trade press puts the industry near half of Loudoun’s property tax revenue. That figure isn’t traced to a county budget line.
    Why
    The “almost 50%” came from Data Center Frontier, which doesn’t point to a budget line, and the county’s own data center tax page gives no percentage. The county’s budget reports the equipment tax by itself: $684.8 million of $2.345 billion in local tax revenue in fiscal 2024. We now lead with that number and grade the larger estimate PLAUSIBLE. The library’s original link, to Straight Arrow News, no longer opens.
    Where
    Straight Talk #4 (/straight-talk/schools-and-taxes), its card on /grid-and-grain, and Module 8 (not yet open).
    Dates
    Caught in a source recheck and fixed on this site November 26, 2026.
  2. Fixed November 26, 2026

    The 8% rural hospital AI figure is PLAUSIBLE, not VERIFIED

    What we said
    VERIFIED Just 8% of critical access and rural community hospitals have put AI-driven predictive analytics in place.
    What it says now
    PLAUSIBLE Becker’s reports that just 8% of critical access and rural community hospitals have put AI-driven predictive analytics in place. It doesn’t name the survey behind the figure, so treat it as a rough size, not a measurement.
    Why
    VERIFIED means a cited source directly supports a figure. The source, Becker’s Hospital Review (Aug. 3, 2026), states the 8% but names no survey or study and links to none, so the figure can’t be checked. The entry also linked to a second address for the same article that doesn’t match it; that link now goes to the article itself.
    Where
    Straight Talk #8 (/straight-talk/rural-benefits) and its card on /grid-and-grain.
    Dates
    Caught while writing Module 6 and fixed on this site November 26, 2026.
  3. Fixed November 26, 2026

    Frederick County rejected the $110 million agreement

    What we said
    Frederick County, Maryland, negotiated more than $110 million in developer-funded benefits.
    What it says now
    In Frederick County, Maryland, a developer offered $110 million in community benefits in exchange for an eight-year freeze on zoning changes that could affect its campus. After public comment, the county executive rejected the agreement on Sept. 14, 2026, and extended a moratorium on new data center applications to July 1, 2027.
    Why
    The agreement was announced on Sept. 1, 2026, and our library recorded the announcement. Two weeks later the county executive rejected it, after residents objected that it gave up local zoning authority (WYPR, Sept. 15, 2026). Our sentence was true on Sept. 2 and misleading after Sept. 14.
    Where
    Straight Talk #7 (/straight-talk/nothing-we-can-do) and its card on /grid-and-grain.
    Dates
    Rejection reported September 15, 2026. Caught in a source recheck and fixed on this site November 26, 2026.
  4. Fixed November 26, 2026

    “Bench Tests” became “Field Tests”

    What we said
    Bench Test: A measurement on a hardware test rig, or a rural AI story. (Shortened here.)
    Every module ships with a long read, an audio version, a five-question self-check, a bench-test video, and a one-page County Kit.
    What it says now
    Field Test: A measurement you can repeat with a plug-in or handheld meter, a phone app, or public data. Or a rural AI story.
    Every module ships with a long read, an audio version, a five-question self-check, a Field Test video, and a one-page County Kit.
    Why
    On November 21, 2026, we changed how Grid & Grain proves a concept. Grid & Grain builds no hardware. Every proof is now a measurement anyone can repeat with off-the-shelf tools or public data, because a reading on a familiar appliance is one you can check yourself. The old wording promised something we no longer do.
    Where
    The weekly formats and the Field Guide description on /grid-and-grain.
    Dates
    Decided November 21, 2026. Fixed on this site November 26, 2026. That is five days, slower than our 48-hour standard. This log starts with it for that reason.
