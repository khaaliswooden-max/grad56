Straight Talk · #9 · What people say

“Data center power use will double forever.”

Our verdict

Plan in ranges.

Efficiency once kept data center power use nearly flat while computing grew. AI changed that, and forecasts now span almost a factor of two.

What the evidence shows

  • VERIFIEDFrom 2010 to 2018, global data center electricity use rose only about 6% while computing grew about 6.5 times, because efficiency improved so fast.

    Source: Masanet et al. (2020), “Recalibrating global data center energy-use estimates,” Science

  • VERIFIEDAI broke that balance. Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory's 2028 projection for U.S. data centers runs from 6.7% to 12% of U.S. electricity.

    Source: Shehabi et al. (2024), Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory
Where the critics are right

Growth is real. A utility that plans only for the low case can be caught short, and one that plans only for the high case can overbuild and bill members for it. PLAUSIBLE

What to ask for, in writing

  • Low, base, and high demand scenarios in any utility plan.
  • Who pays if the high case doesn't happen.
