Straight Talk · #6 · What people say

“AI already uses most of America's electricity.”

Our verdict

Wrong nationally; the local share is what matters.

Data centers used 4.4% of U.S. electricity in 2023. But they cluster, so one utility's share can be far larger.

What the evidence shows

  • VERIFIEDU.S. data centers used about 4.4% of U.S. electricity in 2023. Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory's range for 2028 is 6.7% to 12%, nearly a factor of two, because no one knows how fast AI will grow.

    Source: Shehabi et al. (2024), United States Data Center Energy Usage Report, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory

  • VERIFIEDNearly half of U.S. data center capacity sits in five regional clusters.

    Source: International Energy Agency (2025), Energy and AI. Cited from the Grid & Grain library; the IEA page would not load for our recheck on Nov. 26, 2026.
Where the critics are right

A few percent nationally can be a large share of one utility's peak demand. Where one project would be a big part of the local load, the local numbers are what matter. PLAUSIBLE

What to ask for, in writing

  • Ask your utility or co-op what share of its peak demand the project would be.
