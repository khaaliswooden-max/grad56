Straight Talk · #10 · What people say

“These deals are done in secret.”

Our verdict

Often right.

Local officials and residents have been asked to sign nondisclosure agreements, and secrecy is a leading driver of opposition.

What the evidence shows

  • VERIFIEDLocal officials and residents have been asked to sign nondisclosure agreements about proposed projects, and a political scientist at Washington University in St. Louis describes closed-door negotiation as one of the drivers of opposition.

    Source: Iowa Public Radio, Edgell (Sept. 2, 2026)
Where the critics are right

They're right. Deals are often negotiated behind closed doors, and that secrecy feeds opposition (Iowa Public Radio, above). VERIFIED

What to ask for, in writing

  • A transparency clause: the end user named before any vote.
  • A public hearing before any vote.
  • No nondisclosure agreements for elected officials.
