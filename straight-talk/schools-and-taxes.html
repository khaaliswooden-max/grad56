Straight Talk · #4 · What people say

“It will pay for our schools.”

Our verdict

Partly right.

Data centers can carry a big share of a county's tax base. Tax abatements can give much of it away.

What the evidence shows

  • VERIFIEDIn Loudoun County, Virginia, data centers generate almost 50% of property tax revenue, according to analysis of the county's proposed FY 2026 budget.

    Source: Data Center Frontier (Feb. 14, 2025)

  • VERIFIEDEl Paso, Texas, agreed to an 80% property tax abatement over 35 years for a $1.5 billion data center.

    Source: Brookings, Turner Lee and West (Jan. 29, 2026)

  • PLAUSIBLEThe money a school district sees depends on the abatement terms and on how state school-funding formulas treat new local revenue.

Where the critics are right

Headline tax figures often leave out abatements. If the deal gives most of the tax away, the schools may see far less than promised.

What to ask for, in writing

  • Publish every abatement term before the vote.
  • Publish the net revenue to each taxing district, school board first, year by year.
  • A clawback if promised investment or jobs don't happen.
