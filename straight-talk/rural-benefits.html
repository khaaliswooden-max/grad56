Straight Talk · #8 · What people say

“AI does nothing for rural people.”

Our verdict

Wrong, but adoption is thin.

AI tools are already easing the load on doctors at some rural hospitals. But few rural hospitals use them yet.

What the evidence shows

  • VERIFIEDAt Central Montana Medical Center, a 25-bed critical access hospital, ambient AI scribing cut physicians' after-hours charting so much that some said they would consider leaving if the tool were taken away.

    Source: Becker's Hospital Review (Aug. 3, 2026)

  • VERIFIEDJust 8% of critical access and rural community hospitals have put AI-driven predictive analytics in place.

    Source: Becker's Hospital Review (June 15, 2026)
Where the critics are right

A data center down the road doesn't, by itself, put AI tools in the local clinic. PLAUSIBLE And adoption is thin: 8% is not many hospitals.

What to ask for, in writing

  • Tie any local data center deal to broadband for the hospital and schools.
  • Ask for AI-readiness funding or training for the hospital and schools as part of a benefit agreement.
