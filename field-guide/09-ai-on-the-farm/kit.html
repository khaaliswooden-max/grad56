Why these questions

  • Cost every year. Federal auditors list up-front cost as a main obstacle to precision agriculture, especially for farms with limited capital (GAO, Jan. 2024). VERIFIED A subscription is a cost whether or not the crop comes in.
  • Measure your own signal. In 2025, 85% of U.S. farms had internet access, 74% through a cellular data plan (USDA NASS, Aug. 2025). VERIFIED That describes the farm, not the far corner of a field. A phone speed test is a PLAUSIBLE reading, and it’s from the spot that matters.
  • With no signal. A decision tied to a moving machine can’t wait on a round trip, so it has to be made on the machine (Module 9, Rung 4). Find out which of the tool’s jobs are that kind.
  • The data terms come from the farm data principles as explained by Ohio State University Extension: collection and access, choice, portability, limits on sale, retention, termination, and a bar on anti-competitive use (Fulton, Port, and Colley, 2018). VERIFIED Those are industry principles, not law; the agreement is what binds.
  • Landlords and applicators. The same guide notes that ownership is unclear when several parties work the same ground, and advises spelling it out in the lease or contract. VERIFIED
  • Other brands. Without common standards, one maker’s equipment and records may not work with another’s (GAO, Jan. 2024). VERIFIED
