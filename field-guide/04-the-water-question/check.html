The Water Question: five questions

Answer from memory first. Four right is a pass, and you can retake it as often as you like. Sign in to save your score toward the Field Certificate.

  1. QUESTION 1What decides most of a data center’s on-site water use?

    Why: Evaporative systems can use up to millions of gallons a day; a dry, closed-loop design of the same size can use about what a few dozen homes do. Ask which kind before you argue about size.

  2. QUESTION 2Why does an evaporative cooling system use so much water?

    Why: Evaporation is the cooling mechanism, the way sweat cools skin. About 80% of the water taken in evaporates (Environmental and Energy Study Institute, June 2025).

  3. QUESTION 3For a town’s water system, which number about a proposed data center matters most?

    Why: Water systems are built for the worst day. A data center’s peak day can be several times its average, and it lands when everyone else’s demand is highest too. Ask for both numbers.

  4. QUESTION 4Where is most of the water behind a U.S. data center actually used?

    Why: About 75% of the footprint is indirect, at power plants (Siddik and colleagues, 2021). Berkeley Lab’s 2023 estimate puts upstream water at about twelve times the on-site water.

  5. QUESTION 5Which belongs in a water permit or contract for a data center?

    Why: A design on paper can change. Numbers in a permit can be enforced: cooling type, peak-day and average-day gallons, the source, and what gets cut back under drought restrictions.

Answer all five questions first.
