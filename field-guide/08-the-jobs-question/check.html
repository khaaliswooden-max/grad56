The Jobs Question: five questions

Answer from memory first. Four right is a pass, and you can retake it as often as you like. Sign in to save your score toward the Field Certificate.

  1. QUESTION 1How do a data center’s construction jobs differ from its operating jobs?

    Why: A big crew builds the place and leaves. A small skilled staff runs it for years. Both are real, but they’re different promises and shouldn’t be added into one number.

  2. QUESTION 2According to Georgia Tech researchers, how many permanent workers do rural data centers typically employ?

    Why: Often fewer than 100, with many specialized services brought in from outside the county. Counties outside metro areas saw few measurable gains beyond a small drop in unemployment.

  3. QUESTION 3Why did Brookings researchers compare counties that got data centers with counties where a project was canceled?

    Why: Developers pick places that are already doing well. Comparing built projects with canceled ones separates what the data center did from what was coming anyway.

  4. QUESTION 4What’s the typical way to become an electrician?

    Why: An apprenticeship is a paid job with classroom instruction alongside. Median pay for electricians was $63,190 in May 2025 (Bureau of Labor Statistics), and veterans can use the GI Bill during one.

  5. QUESTION 5One battery in a chain of eight is dead. Using the half-split method, how many readings find it?

    Why: Measure half the chain; the half that reads low holds the bad one. Split that half, then split again: eight to four to two to one. It’s how technicians find a fault without checking every part.

Answer all five questions first.
