Large-load tariff checklist

For a co-op annual meeting, a utility commission comment, or a county hearing. Hold the draft tariff or contract next to this list and check each term it already covers. Nothing you check is saved or sent.

Covers 0 of 66 still to ask for in writing.

Why these terms

Grid upgrades for one giant customer can cost millions. Without these terms, if that customer leaves early or uses less than it reserved, the people already on the system can end up paying for what was built. PLAUSIBLE

Who already does this

  • VERIFIEDAs of May 2026, 23 states had approved at least one large-load tariff.

    Source: Columbia Law School, Sabin Center for Climate Change Law (June 2, 2026), citing the Edison Electric Institute

  • VERIFIEDIn August 2026, Arizona regulators approved a process for six electric co-ops that makes large-load customers cover all the costs of serving them, including new transmission.

    Source: Arizona Corporation Commission (Aug. 13, 2026)
